Notification route

CRAB sends you a short notice when something needs you: a new assignment, items waiting for your decision, a deadline you own, temporary access you can renew, or (where your organisation enables it) a confirmed severity or phase change. A notice says only what kind of item it is and the incident ID. It never contains incident details and never approves anything. Sign in to CRAB to act.

Your route is not used to sign in, recover an account or verify who you are.
